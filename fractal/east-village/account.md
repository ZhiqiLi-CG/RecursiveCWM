## Calibrated mental image
This is an inhabited medieval rural district in a detailed, faceted strategy-map miniature style, with irregularly spaced timber-and-plaster farm buildings, working-looking mills, a market court, and three separate lakeside houses. A complete instance has closed wall volumes, full thick gable roofs and end walls, chimneys, framed mill towers with attached slatted rotors, a round masonry well, supported awnings, solid goods and animals, and usable connections to the surrounding lanes. The reference calibrates this toward dark warm timber, ivory plaster, pale and dark straw, warm tile and distinctive slate-blue roofs, with open ground between groups and much more variation in silhouette than the inherited blocks.
