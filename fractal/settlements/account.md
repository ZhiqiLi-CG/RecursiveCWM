# Settlements — depth 1 account

## Calibrated mental image
This is a small, inhabited medieval strategy-map diorama, rendered as detailed faceted miniatures: timber and plaster villages, working farm windmills, and a separate adobe settlement linked by pale earth lanes. A complete instance has buildings with four walls and full roofs, attached working-looking mill sails, market goods and enclosed farm spaces, and roads that connect entrances and cross water on supported structures. The reference calibrates the architecture toward varied rust-red tile, light/dark straw and blue slate, stout dark timber framing, low parapeted adobe roofs, irregular house orientations and deliberately open courtyard space.
