This is a small working farmyard in a softly lit, faceted medieval miniature: an old dark-roofed timber barn, a lower boarded shed, an ivory canvas-roofed outbuilding, and a loosely scattered flock. A complete farmyard has enclosed, roofed buildings with working doors, structural posts, rear walls and animal bodies supported on legs; the reference calibrates this instance toward compact proportions, weathered warm wood, simple pitched roofs and open space between the three buildings. Hidden surfaces continue those same materials and construction, without introducing unsupported extra enclosures or landscape.
