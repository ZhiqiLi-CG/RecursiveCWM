A complete instance has framed cloth with thickness, usable stall counters and backs, wheeled cart bodies with axles and drawbars, and grounded posts; the reference calibrates this to a sparse ring along the green's edge, with an open center. Hidden sides use the same quiet materials and practical construction without adding a second market behind the visible one.
