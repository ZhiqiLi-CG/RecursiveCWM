A complete instance has enclosed barns and sheds, usable access, roofs on both sides, grounded animals and an attached mill rotor, with lawn separating the working buildings. The reference calibrates that mental image to irregular building orientations, a dark upper barn, a pale central shed, an ivory canvas building, an intersecting tiled house, the vivid blue mill, and a low stone-based thatch barn in front.
